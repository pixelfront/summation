# summation

[Privacy notice](https://pixelfront.github.io/summation/privacy.html)

## Install on your phone

Adding the app to your home screen gives it its own icon. It opens full screen like a normal app and keeps working offline.

### iPhone

1. Open https://pixelfront.github.io/summation/ in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow). On newer iOS versions it may be inside the ⋯ menu.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Always open it from the new home screen icon.

### Android

1. Open the same link in Chrome.
2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**), then **Install**.
3. Open it from the new icon.

In Samsung Internet, use the menu, then **Add page to**, then **Home screen**.

### Good to know

- Open it once while you're online. After that it works without internet.
- Your sheets are saved only on that phone. If you delete the app or clear the browser's data, they're gone, so use Export to keep copies.
- On iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own saved sheets, separate from Safari's.
- Voice dictation needs a connection and uses your phone's own speech service, which may send audio to Apple or Google.
- When there's an update, an update banner appears. Tap it to get the new version.
